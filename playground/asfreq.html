asfreq — tsb playground

Convert a datetime-indexed Series or DataFrame to a specified frequency. Mirrors pandas.Series.asfreq and pandas.DataFrame.asfreq. Unlike resample (which groups and aggregates), asfreq simply reindexes onto a regular grid at the new frequency — upsampled gaps are null unless method or fillValue is given, and downsampled rows not on the new grid are dropped.
